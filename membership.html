Frequently Asked Questions

Can I cancel my subscription anytime?
Yes, you can cancel your subscription at any time. Your access will continue until the end of your billing period, and you will not be charged again.
What happens after my free trial?
After your 14-day free trial, you will be automatically subscribed to the plan you selected. We will send you a reminder 3 days before your trial ends.
Can I upgrade or downgrade my plan?
Absolutely! You can upgrade or downgrade at any time. Upgrades take effect immediately, while downgrades take effect at the next billing cycle.
Is my data secure?
Yes. We use industry-standard encryption to protect your personal and fitness data. We never sell your data to third parties.
